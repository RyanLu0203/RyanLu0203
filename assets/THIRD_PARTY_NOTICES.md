# Asset sources

- `stack-light.svg` and `stack-dark.svg`: [Skill Icons](https://github.com/tandpfun/skill-icons), MIT license. The license is retained in `skill-icons-LICENSE.txt`.
- `snake-light.svg` and `snake-dark.svg`: generated from RyanLu0203's public contribution calendar by [Platane/snk](https://github.com/Platane/snk).
- Hero, typing, wave dividers, direction badges, project cards and public statistics are repository-local SVG graphics. They contain no JavaScript or external font loads. Their editable sources are in `scripts/`.
- The original approved raster banner remains in `profile-banner.png`.
